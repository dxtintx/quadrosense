QuadroSense App: advanced DualSense controls.

Controller Emulation: Emulates standard legacy and modern PC gamepads to make third-party controllers work across almost any PC game via USB or Bluetooth.
Supported Controllers: Works with a wide variety of wired and wireless gamepads equipped with adaptive triggers, touchpads, and motion sensors, along with partial support for VR and accessibility controllers.
Virtual Native Controller Emulation: Creates a virtual native advanced controller over both USB and Bluetooth so games with built-in support recognize it directly, passing through native adaptive triggers, haptics, rumble, and lighting.
Adaptive Triggers Customization: Lets you configure custom trigger resistance, tension, vibration, and dynamic trigger modes for the left and right triggers.
Haptics and Rumble Conversion: Converts standard game rumble or system audio waves into high-definition haptic feedback, and allows you to adjust vibration intensity.
Bluetooth Audio and Haptic Feedback: Enables full wireless audio routing and native haptic feedback over Bluetooth, including support for the built-in controller speaker, headphone jack, auto audio switching, volume sliders, microphone beamforming, and haptic gain controls.
Button Remapping and Macros: Supports full button remapping, turbo modes, hold and toggle actions, action blocks, and custom shortcuts.
Stick, Touchpad, and Gyro Tuning: Provides deadzone adjustments, sensitivity curves, touchpad mouse emulation, and gyroscope motion controls.
RGB and LED Lighting Control: Customizes lightbar colors, player indicator LEDs, microphone mute LEDs, and dynamic lighting effects such as battery status or audio-reactive lighting.
Custom Profiles and Auto Switching: Lets you create, share, color-tag, and automatically switch profiles depending on which game or application is running.
Mod System and Game Integrations: Includes a built-in mod framework that lets games and community plugins directly control adaptive triggers, haptics, and LEDs in real time.
Hardware Diagnostics and Backups: Displays battery level, connection type, Bluetooth signal quality, and hardware stats, alongside automatic profile and settings backups.
Standalone Offline Operation: Runs completely standalone and offline as a lightweight utility with no external launchers, accounts, or internet connection required.
